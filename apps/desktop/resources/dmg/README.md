# DMG background

`background.png` is a 640 × 400 installation background made with the built-in
imagegen tool. Finder supplies the real app and Applications icons; they are not
painted into the image. The packaging configuration places their centers at
`(170, 190)` and `(470, 190)`.

The first generated design used a plain ivory background and a straight coral
arrow. The revised design adds book pages, a red bookmark, and a curved arrow.
It was checked in Finder using a built DMG.

## Generation brief

Generate a warm ivory macOS DMG background for Yomitomo, with restrained coral
accents. Keep two clear icon areas at the left and right of the middle row.
Place a visible arrow between them pointing right, the title “Yomitomo” above,
and “Drag to Applications” below. Do not draw app or folder icons.

Edit this background to make it more interesting and related to reading. Add
layered book pages at the lower left and a folded page with a coral red bookmark
at the upper right. Make the arrow gently curved. Keep the icon areas clear,
the instruction legible, and the composition calm. Preserve the title and
installation instruction.

The final image was resized to the DMG window dimensions with `sips`.
